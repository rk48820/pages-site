Recipe Organizer & Meal Planner

How to Use

  1. Enter a recipe name, ingredients, and instructions in the "Add a Recipe" section.
  2. Click the "Add Recipe" button to save it.
  3. Your saved recipes will appear in the "Saved Recipes" list below.
  4. In the "Meal Planner" section, use the dropdowns to assign a saved recipe to each day of the week.
  5. Changes are saved automatically in your browser (using local storage).
